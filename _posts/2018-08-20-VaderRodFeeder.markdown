description: |
  **Motivation:** The VADER metal 3D printing machine can only print from wire spools. Using rods could save significant cost; improve material abailability and secure supply chain; and allow for new alloys to be used in printing.
  **Objective:** Design and demonstrate a rod feeding concept for metal AM machines that can:
  - Store at least thirty 1ft. rods of 1/8" dia.
  - Feed fast enough to keep up with demand.
  - Be controlled by digital signal from AM machine.

  **Design:** I was project manager of a team of 6 graduate students and contributed to mechanical and electrical design. Budget was $2500 with a 9 month timeline. After collecting voice of customer and agreeing on requirements, we took an iterative design approach emphasizing rapid prototyping and Design Of Experiments to focus in on the optimal concept. In the final product, rods are pre-loaded into the storage hopper by the machine operator at shift start. When material is required, the AM machine calls for a rod and the device uses an agitator to cause the rods to flow from the hopper into the feeder which can then project the rods directly into the crucible at the required speed. 
  **Outcome:** We successfully created the rod feeding concept, on time and under budget, and demonstrated the device operation at ImagineRIT. Customer was very satisfied with delivered product.
