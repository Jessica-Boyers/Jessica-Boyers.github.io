description: >
  The HMX SDU1 Ammunition Storage Display Unit is a firearm accessory for lever action rifles or other tube-fed firearms to display the number of rounds remaining in the magazine. Inspired by science-fiction media, this accessory provides a useful and aesthetic reference for recreational, sport, and competition shooters to know the state of their magazine without disassembling or clearing the firearm.
  The device mounts to the normal optical rail of the firearm and is a drop-in self contained replacement for the magazine of a Henry Model X, and can be hotswapped or uninstalled freely. I designed and developed this product and sell it at Sovereigness Designs.
