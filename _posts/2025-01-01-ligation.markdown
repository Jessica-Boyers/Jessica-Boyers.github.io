description: |
  **Motivation:** Polymer ligation is a +$100M market for Teleflex Medical. The predicate product had been unchanged since the late '90s and all relevant patents expired. Market research over the last 2 decades identified several pain points and unsolved clinical needs in the use of polymer clip systems.

  **Objective:** Redesign the entire system (Clip, Cartridge, Applier, Remover) to satisfy new needs while preserving key user experience.

  **Design:** I was technical lead of the 'Next Gen Hem-o-lok' project to accomplish this full system redesign. Alongside a team, we developed novel new mechanical approaches to the Applier and Remover while using complaint data analysis to understand lessons learned from the predicate device and proactively incorporate solutions.

  **Outcome:** Developed, tested, validated, and transfered a new full system design that achieved the new needs, renewed patents, and resolved the two most common complaints.
